Any rule keyed on a player's home sport (e.g. NEO-426 "No teams in this sport") behaves
differently in mixed sets (Allen & Ginter, Goodwin Champions, Topps Heritage celebrity
inserts) than its author expects.

**Why:** a person CREATED in the wizard is born with the set's sport unless the operator
uses "Sport for this name"; Moby created in an A&G Baseball set becomes a Baseball player
and the default silently does not fire (he lands back in "Cards with no team"). And copy
saying "its player's sport has none" reads as "Baseball has no teams" on a Baseball set.

**How to apply:** in review, (1) ask whether the creation step nudges the sport switch,
(2) demand copy that NAMES the sport ("Non Sport") and the people, never "this sport" /
"their sport" on a screen whose set belongs to another sport, (3) a save-step count must
list who (cap ~5 + "and N more"), see [[rebuild-and-copy-features-checklist]] (counts
without names). House casing for the stamp is "No Team" (set default, "Mark ... No Team").
Hobby word "Non Sport" is the category's real name; keep "sport" as the level word.
